# Contract: category-definition

Shared rules: [document-rules.md](document-rules.md).

## Meaning

A proposed market category: the distinction existing categories fail to communicate, the membership that makes it a real category rather than the venture's feature list, the point of view and language that let buyers recognise and compare members, and the adoption path. Meaning integrates it into the lexicon and messages, Identity handles naming expression, and Engagement executes category-building; they refer to this definition rather than redefine membership.

The category, its name and language are proposed constructions. Owner acceptance of a name is not evidence of market adoption; a claim of existing recognition needs its own basis.

## Identity and scope

At most one instance per venture, at `venture/{v}/strategy/category-definition.md`, present only when positioning-decision activates category design.

## Required properties

- Membership is defined by problem, approach or outcome distinctions, tested on representative included and excluded cases.
- The relation to existing categories is explicit.
- Only the language needed for understanding and choice is defined.
- The adoption path names the initial audience, the understanding or behaviour that must change, the demonstration, the execution owner and an observation of progress.
- The benefit is compared with staying in an existing frame.

## Consumer reliance

Consumers may rely on the membership rules and term meanings as the venture's proposed category. They may not rely on market recognition.

## Acceptance

- Specialist evaluation (design-category): boundaries carry distinctions beyond the venture's features; buyers have a plausible reason to adopt the frame; the adoption burden is explicit.
- Structural (document check).
- The owner accepts it.

## Representation

| Section | Form | Size | Requiredness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Category purpose | Statement | 1–2 sentences | required |
| Definition and boundaries | Table plus prose | One row per test case; up to 2 short paragraphs | required |
| Point of view and language | Prose plus table | Up to 3 short paragraphs; one row per term | required |
| Adoption path | Table | One row per consequential adoption step | required |

**Category purpose:** the distinction the category makes useful to the buyer.

**Definition and boundaries:** the proposed name and membership conditions in prose, the relation to existing categories, and the boundary test cases in the table.

**Point of view and language:** the current problem, the proposed approach, and the necessary terms.
