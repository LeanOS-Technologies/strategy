# Contract: positioning-decision

Shared rules: [document-rules.md](document-rules.md).

## Meaning

The frame in which the target understands the venture's leading value: an existing category, a narrower frame or a new one, with the expectations it creates and why it beats the strongest alternative frame. It states the competitive choice and value, not campaign copy. Meaning and Identity develop expression from it; market and commercial workers keep the same target, advantage and scope; it decides whether a category-definition is needed.

## Identity and scope

One instance per venture, at `venture/{v}/strategy/positioning-decision.md`. Its audience scope stays inside the venture-definition.

## Required properties

- The choice names the target and situation, the chosen frame and the leading comparative value, referencing their records.
- Each consequential expectation the frame creates is checked against the actual offering; a mismatch is named with its resolution.
- The rationale compares the strongest alternative frame and states the education burden.
- Offering basis carries sources and reasoning for predicted buyer interpretations; each consequential interpretation has an action that tests it.
- The frame choice develops and compares the existing frame, a narrower frame and a new category the venture's perspective could lead, and chooses one.

## Consumer reliance

Meaning and Identity may rely on the frame, the leading value and the expectations to preserve. Nobody may rely on predicted buyer interpretation as observed.

## Acceptance

- Specialist evaluation (choose-frame): the frame highlights the important advantage, creates accurate expectations and has a workable education burden for the commercial path.
- Structural (document check): envelope, sections, columns, quoted owner passages, amounts with basis, references.
- The owner accepts it.

## Representation

| Section | Form | Size | Requiredness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positioning choice | Statement | 1–2 sentences | required |
| Frame meaning | Table | One row per consequential expectation | required |
| Choice rationale | Prose | Up to 3 short paragraphs | required |
| Implications | List | One line per material consequence | required |

**Positioning choice:** target and situation, chosen frame, leading comparative value.

**Choice rationale:** strongest alternative frame, why the chosen frame is preferred, education burden, remaining assumption.

**Implications:** for Meaning, Identity, Engagement or offer scope, and the category-definition to write when the new category is chosen or worth developing as a live option.

### Frame meaning columns

| Column | Holds | Kind |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Expectation` | What the buyer will expect inside this frame | reasoning |
| `Offering basis` | What the actual offering provides, with availability | reasoning |
| `Mismatch to resolve` | The mismatch and its resolution, or `none` | factual |
